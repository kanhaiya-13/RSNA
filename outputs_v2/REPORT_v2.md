### 6. Overfitting Trajectory & Turning Point

* **Where Validation Loss Turned Upward:** Across all 5 folds, validation loss reached its global minimum between **Epoch 6 and Epoch 8** (mean: Epoch 7.4, val loss: $0.5016$).
* **Trajectory Relative to Cosine Schedule:** In the 30-epoch schedule, the learning rate decayed toward $1\times 10^{-6}$. Past Epoch ~8–10, validation loss turned upward monotonically to $\approx 0.517$–$0.531$, while training loss continued dropping from $\approx 0.447$ to $\approx 0.325$.
* **Conclusion:** Severe overfitting occurs in the second half of the training schedule. Peak generalisation is attained around Epoch 8–10.

## 7. Input Resolution and Source Data

### 23. Native Image Sizes, Slice Counts, and Information Loss

* **Dataset on Disk:** All 24,371 `.npz` files in [D:/rsna_knee_dataset/](file:///D:/rsna_knee_dataset) are pre-processed to $(24, 224, 224)$ uint8 arrays (total size: 32.37 GB, from Kaggle `barun2104/rsna-knee-mri-processed-3d-volumes`).
* **Original DICOM Dimensions:** Native knee MRI scans typically have matrix sizes of $512 \times 512$ or $384 \times 384$ with 20–40 slices per series.
* **Information Loss:** Resizing from $512 \times 512$ to $224 \times 224$ discards $1 - (224/512)^2 \approx \mathbf{80.86\%}$ of in-plane pixel data. Fixed depth resampling to 24 slices introduces z-axis partial volume averaging.
